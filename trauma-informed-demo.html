🎯 Design Principles

These components implement SAMHSA's six principles of trauma-informed care:

1. Pacing over efficiency - Users move at their own speed through consent steppers and warnings
2. Repair is always possible - Regret buttons provide undo windows to reduce shame
3. Granular consent - Each decision is separate with genuine opt-out options
4. Progressive disclosure - Warnings prepare users before difficult content
5. Exit is always visible - Safe exit button prevents trapped feelings
6. Predictable calm - Consistent design tokens and gentle animations create safety
