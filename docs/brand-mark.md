# Cognitive Engine mark

The symbol shows three routes converging at a decision point and continuing as one clear path. Its geometry echoes the site's diagrams and the product's purpose: turning UX evidence into an actionable intervention. The central point also makes a subtle `E` from the incoming paths, giving the icon a recognizable silhouette without relying on a literal brain or gear.

## Usage

- Master asset: `assets/brand/cognitive-engine-mark.svg`.
- Pair the mark with the live text “Cognitive Engine” in page headers. The text provides the accessible link name; the icon's `alt` should remain empty there.
- Use the mark alone for the favicon or small square placements. Keep it at least 16 × 16 px. In headers, use 34 × 34 px.
- Keep clear space around it equal to roughly one quarter of the mark's width. Do not recolor its paths independently or place it on a busy background.

The colors match the site system: pine `#174B40`, warm paper `#F7F3EA`, and insight coral `#ECA47D`.
