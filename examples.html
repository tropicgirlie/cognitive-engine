Problem

Solution

Investment Choice Paralysis

Finance • Decision-approvals
Problem

Users presented with 50+ investment options spent 12 minutes on average and often made no choice. High abandonment rate.

Solution

Applied Hick's Law to limit initial options to 5, with "Explore More" for advanced users. Added Recognition over Recall for common choices.

Checkout Form Abandonment

E-commerce • Data-entry-forms
Problem

68% of users abandoned checkout due to long, intimidating forms. Users reported feeling overwhelmed by required fields.

Solution

Applied Miller's Law to chunk form into 3 sections, Progressive Disclosure for optional fields, and Fitts's Law for larger touch targets.

Medication App Errors

Healthcare • Data-entry-forms
Problem

Elderly users frequently made dosage errors due to small input fields and unclear labels. 15% error rate in medication logging.

Solution

Applied Fitts's Law for larger targets, added Recognition over Recall with common dosages, and used clear error prevention.
